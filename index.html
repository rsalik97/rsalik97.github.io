Who I Am

One person who can design the logo, animate it and cut the film around it

I can take a project from the first sketch to the final export without handing it on.

I'm Rokhan Salik, a graphic designer and video producer with more than ten years in marketing and television. On documentaries and commercials I've handled concept, script, directing, editing and arranging the broadcast with TV channels.

I've lived in Magdeburg since 2022 and hold a telc B2 certificate in German. I also build my own tools: I'm developing Glassmetry Pro, a liquid-glass effect plugin for After Effects.
